## Context

At Benevity, one of the core user workflows leaned heavily on manual operations.
The data needed to run it was scattered across Slack, ClickUp, and Salesforce, so
the team stitched things together by hand — slow, error-prone, and impossible to
scale with the user base.

## The problem

Every request meant hunting through three tools and re-keying data. Five-minute
tickets piled up, support and CS staff spent their days on repetitive work, and
5,000+ users felt the friction.

## What I did

- **Mapped every touchpoint** of the workflow end to end to find what was
  repetitive, rule-based, and safe to automate.
- **Defined the data-field mapping** to consolidate the scattered data into a
  single central database — the foundation everything else depended on.
- **Rolled out API integrations and low-code automations in phases**, automating
  the highest-volume manual steps first, measuring, then expanding.

## Outcome

- **Manual workload cut by 70%** (≈40% at first release, rising with later iterations).
- **5-minute tickets became sub-minute or fully automated actions.**
- **~15 support and CS staff freed** for higher-value work.
- **5,000+ users** lost a recurring source of friction.

## Why it mattered

This is the work I love most: unglamorous workflow problems where careful mapping
and the right automation quietly change how a whole team operates.
