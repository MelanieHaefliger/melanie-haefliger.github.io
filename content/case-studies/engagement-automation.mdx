## Context

At an employee-engagement scale-up, the core loop — survey employees, surface
insights, prompt managers to act — leaned heavily on manual operations. The ops
team stitched together exports and reminders by hand, and insights reached
managers slowly.

## The problem

The manual workflow didn't scale with the customer base. It was error-prone, it
buried the ops team, and the lag between survey and action eroded the product's
core value: helping managers respond while feedback was still fresh.

## What I did

- Mapped the end-to-end workflow and found the steps that were repetitive,
  rule-based, and safe to automate.
- Designed an automated engagement loop: triggered insight digests, smart
  defaults, and manager nudges at the right moment.
- Sequenced delivery to de-risk — automating the highest-volume manual steps
  first, measuring, then expanding.

## Outcome

- **~20 manual ops hours/week** freed up.
- **Time-to-insight down ~60%** for managers.
- **Manager adoption up 34%** as insights became timely and actionable.

## Why it mattered

This is the kind of work I love: unglamorous workflow problems where thoughtful
automation quietly changes how a whole team works.
