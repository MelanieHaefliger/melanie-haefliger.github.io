## The problem

Most career-pivot advice is generic and overwhelming. People know they want to
change direction but get stuck turning that feeling into a concrete next step.
The gap isn't motivation — it's a clear, personalized plan.

## What I built

**PivotPilot** is an AI copilot that takes someone from "I think I want to
pivot" to a concrete weekly plan in under five minutes. It maps transferable
skills, suggests realistic target roles, and generates a tailored learning path.

- **Skill mapping** — the model extracts transferable strengths from a free-text
  background and reframes them for a target role.
- **Grounded suggestions** — a small RAG layer keeps role and skill suggestions
  anchored to real, current job-market signals rather than hallucinated advice.
- **Plan generation** — output is a structured, editable weekly plan, not a wall
  of text.

## My role

I built this solo, end to end: problem framing, UX, the LLM/RAG architecture, and
the early go-to-market. It's where my product instincts and hands-on AI work meet.

## What I learned

> The hard part of an AI product isn't the model — it's designing the moments of
> trust around it.

Constraining the model's output into a structured, editable plan did more for
perceived quality than any prompt tweak. Shipping the unglamorous guardrails is
the product.
