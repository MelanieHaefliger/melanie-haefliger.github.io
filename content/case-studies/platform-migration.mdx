## Context

Benevity was sunsetting a legacy platform and moving clients to its successor.
Migrations like this are quietly dangerous: long-standing accounts depend on
configurations that no one fully remembers, and a bad transition risks churn.

## The problem

30 accounts — a mix of enterprise and mid-market — were exposed. The risk wasn't
just technical; it was the hidden, undocumented client setups that could break on
migration and trigger cancellations. Someone had to surface them and make a call.

## What I did

- **Surfaced hidden configurations** by digging into how each account actually
  used the platform, not just how it was documented.
- **Ran a structured Go/No-Go risk assessment**, weighing dependencies, client
  impact, and readiness for each account.
- **Made and communicated the call** on behalf of my function, keeping leadership
  aligned on risks and progress throughout.

## Outcome

- **~$200K ARR retained** across the 30 affected accounts.
- Migration risks identified **early**, before they became cancellations.

## Takeaway

Good migration work is mostly discovery and judgment: find what's hidden, assess
honestly, and decide with the revenue and the user in mind.
