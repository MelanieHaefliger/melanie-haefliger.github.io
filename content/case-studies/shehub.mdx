## Context

SheHub is a women's-empowerment non-profit that needed a real product, not just a
landing page — a live community platform and the internal tooling to run it. I
joined as a volunteer Product Manager to make that happen.

## What I did

- **Ran user research** to understand what the community actually needed.
- **Turned findings into user stories and requirements**, then drove them through
  to a launched, live public platform: [shehub.es](https://shehub.es).
- **Defined the strategy and PRD** for an internal admin tool — now shipped — that
  automates manual community-management tasks.

## Outcome

- A **live public platform** the community uses today.
- An **internal admin tool shipped** that takes manual work off the team's plate.

## Why I included it

It shows the full 0→1 muscle — research, requirements, and shipping — plus the
same automation instinct I bring to my paid work, applied to a cause I care about.
