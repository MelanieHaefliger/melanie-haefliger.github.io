## Context

SheHub is a women's-empowerment non-profit that needed a real product, not just a landing page — a live community platform and the internal tooling to run it. I joined as a volunteer Product Manager to make that happen.

## The problem

Community management was entirely manual. Every new member application meant a round of individual messages across LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and email — then chasing spreadsheets to track who was assigned where. There was no centralised view, no automated handoffs, and every step required someone to do it by hand.

<SheHubBeforeAfter />

## What we built toward

I defined the PRD for an internal admin tool to replace the manual process end-to-end: structured intake, a single pipeline view, and automated actions triggered on approval.

<SheHubFeatures />

## What I did

- **Ran user research** with community members and the admin team to understand exactly where the friction was.
- **Turned findings into user stories and requirements**, then drove them through to a launched, live public platform: [shehub.es](https://shehub.es).
- **Defined the strategy and PRD** for the internal admin tool — now shipped — that automates onboarding emails, Slack access, and project assignments on approval.

## Outcome

- A **live public platform** the community uses today — 1,000+ LinkedIn members, 200+ Instagram followers.
- An **internal admin tool shipped** that removes the manual handoffs from the team's plate.

> 0→1 — research, requirements, and shipping — plus the same automation instinct I bring to my paid work, applied to a cause I care about.
