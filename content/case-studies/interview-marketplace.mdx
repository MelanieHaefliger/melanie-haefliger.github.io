> This is an anonymized interview case study, included to show how I think — not
> a shipped product.

## The prompt

Design a growth and trust strategy for a two-sided marketplace facing a classic
cold-start problem: not enough supply to attract demand, and not enough demand to
attract supply.

## How I approached it

- **Pick a side, pick a niche.** Concentrate on one geography and one category to
  reach liquidity in a beachhead before expanding.
- **Manufacture the short side.** Where supply lags, fill it deliberately
  (concierge, subsidies, or owned inventory) until organic supply takes over.
- **Engineer trust early.** Reviews, guarantees, and clear dispute resolution
  matter more than features at this stage — trust is the real product.

## How I'd measure it

Liquidity (match rate and time-to-match) within the beachhead, not vanity
top-line GMV. Expand only once the beachhead clears a liquidity bar.

## Why I included this

It shows the muscle I bring to ambiguous, strategic problems: structure first,
sequence deliberately, and measure the thing that actually predicts success.
